# Signal Recipe Image Style Guide

## Style: Minimalist Hand-Drawn Watercolor Sketches

### Core Characteristics
- **Line work**: Thin, hand-drawn pen outlines in warm brown/sienna tone
- **Fill**: Mostly white/empty with subtle watercolor washes
- **Color**: One or two pops of color per illustration (warm orange, muted green, soft red)
- **Background**: Clean white, no background elements
- **Composition**: Centered, simple, single subject (bowl, plate, glass, wrap)
- **Texture**: Slight hand-drawn imperfection, organic feel
- **Detail level**: Minimal — suggest ingredients rather than photorealistic rendering

### Prompt Template
```
Hand-drawn minimalist watercolor sketch of [DISH DESCRIPTION] on a clean white background. 
Thin brown pen outlines with subtle [COLOR] watercolor accents. Simple, elegant food illustration 
style with organic hand-drawn quality. No text, no labels, no background elements.
```

### Color Palette by Phase
- **Menstrual**: Warm reds, deep orange (iron-rich warmth)
- **Follicular**: Fresh greens, light yellow (spring energy)
- **Ovulatory**: Bright orange, mango yellow (peak vitality)
- **Luteal**: Deep amber, warm brown (grounding comfort)
