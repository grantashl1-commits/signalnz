Mediterranean Retreat

Foxterra-Inspired Outdoor Living — Pool, Spa, Sauna & Alfresco Kitchen

Material Palette

Travertine — Terrace & Pool Coping
Limestone — Retaining Wall & Features
Pool Water — Salt Chlorinated Blue
Terracotta — Pots, Accents & Trim
Hardwood Timber — Pergola & Sauna
Greenery — Lavender, Olives & Climbers

Design Notes

Pool Slide integrates into existing bank — no excavation of bank required; slide carved into terrace wall
Travertine terrace lays directly on existing lawn level — raised slightly to create visual separation
Pergola over kitchen provides shade and defines the alfresco zone without enclosing it
Sauna positioned to capture morning sun and allow easy access to spa pool beside it
🏊

Swimming Pool

11m × 5m salt pool with travertine coping, underwater LED lighting, and mosaic waterline tile. Steps at house end. Pool slide entry from bank wall.

🌊

Pool Slide

Curved limestone-clad slide built into the existing bank terrace wall. Water-fed for continuous flow. Timber platform entry at top of bank with handrail.

♨️

Spa Pool

1.8m circular hydrotherapy spa, set into travertine terrace. Bench seating for 4–5, jet system, integrated heating. Adjacent to sauna for contrast therapy.

🌡️

Outdoor Sauna

Thermally modified timber sauna with stone base, double-glazed window, cedar interior. Bench capacity 6. Positioned to step straight into spa or rinse shower.

🍳

Outdoor Kitchen

L-shaped limestone island with integrated gas grill, sink with hot/cold, outdoor fridge, and stone bench. Shaded by hardwood pergola with climbing greenery.

🪨

Travertine Terrace

Large-format (600×600) travertine pavers in honed finish throughout. Wraps pool, spa, and kitchen zones. Mediterranean warmth — durable, non-slip, frost-resistant.
